## Three differences verified after the audit request

1. **p101: mammography every two years ages 50-74.** USPSTF final recommendation, 30 April 2024, recommends biennial mammography ages 40-74 in its applicable population. Action so far: screening-age card deferred; other breast cards included. This is a verified difference in recommendations, not proof of which answer a particular exam form expects.
   Source read directly: https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/breast-cancer-screening
2. **pp61,78: clarithromycin/amoxicillin/PPI first-line for H. pylori; bismuth quadruple if it fails.** ACG 2024 guidance recommends optimized bismuth quadruple for 14 days when susceptibility is unknown and advises against clarithromycin triple without proven sensitivity. Action so far: regimen cards deferred. Verified against the issuing society's guideline summary, not the full journal guideline.
   Source read directly: https://gi.org/journals-publications/ebgi/schoenfeld_sep2024/
3. **p36: outpatient asthma begins with albuterol; add ICS for weekly symptoms.** GINA 2026 says adults, adolescents and children 6-11 should not receive SABA alone and should receive ICS-containing treatment. Action so far: rigid outpatient ladder deferred; diagnosis and mechanism cards included. This is a verified difference from GINA, with exam-specific framing still a separate question.
   Source read directly: https://ginasthma.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/GINA-Summary-Guide-2026-WEB-WMS.pdf

## Additional checks completed after the first audit list

These were also checked after the audit question, not before deferral:

- **p42 latent-TB nine-month INH rule:** CDC/NTCA2020 guideline prefers3-4-month rifamycin-based regimens. Six/nine-month INH remain alternatives, so nine-month INH is not simply "wrong"; it was overbroad to label it that way. Full guideline fetched: https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/69/rr/rr6901a1.htm
- **p46 empiric antibiotics pending throat culture:** CDC clinical guidance updated18 November2025 says children older than3 with negative RADT should have backup culture and a mechanism to start antibiotics if that culture is positive. This differs from the PDF's blanket presumptive antibiotic advice. Direct page fetched: https://www.cdc.gov/group-a-strep/hcp/clinical-guidance/strep-throat.html
- **p70 HCV IgM/IgG staging shortcut:** CDC testing guidance dated31 January2025 uses HCV antibody with reflex RNA. Antibody alone can mean current, past resolved or false positivity; detectable RNA establishes current infection. This supports not using the PDF shortcut as a diagnostic rule, although this page is not a direct immunoglobulin-class study. Direct page fetched: https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-c/hcp/diagnosis-testing/
